Vox for NVDA users

Vox follows NVDA's conventions, so most of your keys and habits work unchanged. This page lists what is the same, what has another name and what is different. Back to the user guide.

Starting out

  • Bring your settings. The first time Vox starts it offers to bring over your NVDA voice, rate, pitch, volume, punctuation, capitals, typing echo, keyboard layout, braille settings, your own input gestures and your speech dictionaries; later, choose Import settings from NVDA or JAWS in the Vox menu. Vox shows what it brought over and what it could not before anything changes.
  • The NVDA key is the Vox key: Insert or Caps Lock, pressed twice quickly for its own function.
  • The same layouts. Vox's desktop and laptop keyboard layouts are NVDA's; choose one in Settings, Keyboard (Insert+Ctrl+G). The keyboard reference lists every command.

Ideas you know

NVDA ideas and their Vox counterparts
In NVDAIn Vox
Browse mode and focus modeThe same, with the same keys and sounds; Vox also starts web applications such as mail and chat in focus mode.
Review cursor and object navigationThe same, on the numeric keypad (or Insert with the arrow keys on a laptop); review modes are the navigator object, the focused text and the screen.
NVDA menuThe Vox menu, Insert+N, also on the Vox icon in the notification area.
Settings, configuration profiles, input gesturesThe same: Insert+Ctrl+G, Insert+Ctrl+P, and Input gestures in the Vox menu.
Speech dictionariesVox's pronunciation dictionaries: one for every voice and one per voice, imported from NVDA's.
Add-ons and the add-on storeVox has its own add-ons and store (Vox menu). NVDA add-ons do not run in Vox; most popular ones' features are built in (Office, Teams, Zoom, OCR, image descriptions, remote access).
NVDA RemoteRemote access, in the Vox menu; Insert+F11 switches control.
Speech viewer, sleep mode, input helpThe same: the speech viewer is in the Vox menu (Insert+Shift+V), sleep mode Insert+Shift+S, input help Insert+1.
Speech mode (NVDA+S)Insert+S pauses all speech until pressed again; there are no beeps mode.
Python console, developer scratchpadNone: Vox add-ons are .NET assemblies built against the Vox SDK.

What Vox adds

  • Insert+Shift+D presses the Reject or Close button of a cookie banner or dialog over the page.
  • Insert+Shift+I summarises the page; Insert+Ctrl+I has AI summarise it, and Insert+Ctrl+M moves to the main content.
  • Insert+G describes a picture; Insert+Alt+G asks a question about the window in front.
  • If you also know JAWS, the JAWS keyboard layouts add its keys: see Vox for JAWS users.
