Vox for JAWS users

Most of what you do in JAWS you can do in Vox, often with the same keys. This page maps the keys and ideas you know to Vox's. Back to the user guide.

Starting out

  • Bring your settings. The first time Vox starts it offers to bring over your JAWS rate, punctuation, typing echo, your own key assignments and your dictionary entries; later, choose Import settings from NVDA or JAWS in the Vox menu. Vox shows what it brought over and what it could not before anything changes.
  • Use the JAWS keys. Choose the JAWS desktop or JAWS laptop keyboard layout in the welcome wizard (3 or 4) or in Settings, Keyboard (Insert+Ctrl+G). They are Vox's own layouts with the common JAWS keys added; where JAWS and Vox disagree, the JAWS key wins. The JAWS keyboard reference lists every command's keys in these layouts.
  • The JAWS key is the Vox key: Insert, or Caps Lock on a laptop. Press it twice quickly for its own function.

What Vox adds

  • Insert+Shift+D presses the Reject or Close button of a cookie banner or dialog over the page.
  • Insert+Shift+I summarises the page; Insert+Ctrl+I has AI summarise it.
  • Insert+Alt+G asks a question about the window in front.
